# Marathon Training Meal Plan (1-Day)

---

## Macronutrient Targets
- **Calories:** 2,800-3,000 kcal
- **Carbs:** 55-60% (385-450g) — fuel for endurance
- **Protein:** 15-20% (105-150g) — recovery & maintenance
- **Fat:** 20-25% (65-85g) — satiety & hormone health

---

## Daily Meal Plan

### **BREAKFAST** (7:00 AM) — 600 kcal
- 2 cups oatmeal with honey (70g dry oats)
- 1 banana + 1 tbsp almond butter
- 1 cup whole milk
- **Macros:** 95g carbs | 20g protein | 12g fat

### **SNACK 1** (10:00 AM) — 300 kcal
- Energy bar (or homemade: dates + almonds)
- 1 large apple
- 200ml sports drink (6% carbs)
- **Macros:** 70g carbs | 8g protein | 5g fat

### **LUNCH** (1:00 PM) — 700 kcal
- 200g grilled chicken breast
- 1.5 cups white/brown rice
- Steamed vegetables (broccoli, carrots) with 1 tsp olive oil
- **Macros:** 85g carbs | 45g protein | 8g fat

### **SNACK 2** (4:00 PM) — Pre-run fueling — 250 kcal
- 50g bagel with 1 tbsp jam
- 1 orange
- **Macros:** 55g carbs | 8g protein | 1g fat

### **DINNER** (7:00 PM) — 400 kcal
- 150g salmon (or lean beef)
- 150g sweet potato
- Green salad with 1 tbsp olive oil dressing
- **Macros:** 35g carbs | 35g protein | 12g fat

### **EVENING SNACK** (if needed) — 150 kcal
- Greek yogurt (150g) + granola
- **Macros:** 20g carbs | 15g protein | 3g fat

- Sports drink (6-8% carbs) during long runs
- Electrolyte drink post-run
